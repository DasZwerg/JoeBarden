v3.2.3 BETA

Summary
- Groq (Llama/Mixtral) integrated as a cross-provider AI fallback. If all Gemini models fail, the bot automatically retries the request through Groq before giving up.

🤖 NEW: GROQ AI FALLBACK
- Added generate_content_with_groq() and a top-level generate_content() dispatcher.
- All AI commands (#ask, #dj, #bard, #roastdj) now go through generate_content(), which tries Gemini first and falls through to Groq only on full Gemini failure (outage, key issues, all models exhausted).
- Groq quota errors do NOT trigger fallback — daily quota exhaustion still surfaces the BARD_QUOTA_MESSAGE correctly.
- Groq models tried in order: llama-3.3-70b-versatile → llama-3.1-8b-instant → mixtral-8x7b-32768.
- Groq is optional: set GROQ_API_KEY in .env to enable. If absent, bot warns at startup and skips Groq silently.
- Free Groq key: https://console.groq.com — email signup, no billing, ~60 seconds. Difficulty: 1/5.
- AI availability guards updated across all commands to check both Gemini and Groq, not just Gemini.
- openai>=1.0.0 added to requirements.txt (used as the Groq API compatibility layer).
